Pelmanists

Memory games and VR interventions for older adults.

AI 380: Human-AI Interaction, Fall 2026, Willamette University

Project vision

Memory naturally becomes harder to rely on as we age. Many older adults do not have an easy, low-pressure way to keep working their memory. We are currently planning to build a VR memory-training game, based on the Simon; the classic sequence-recall game, designed specifically for older adults: large targets, high-contrast visuals, gentle pacing, and forgiving error recovery, so the challenge stays in the memory task, not in fighting the interface.

Over the term, we'll talk with older adults directly to understand what memory challenges actually look like in their day-to-day lives. After that, we'll move from paper prototypes to a working 9-screen VR experience built and tested on the Meta Quest 3. Later milestones take the concept further into a conversational AI assistant, with the goal of a system that's genuinely usable and useful for the people it's meant to help.
