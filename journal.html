Process / Journal

Weekly updates on what we did, what we learned, and what's next. Newest entries first.

  1. Week 2, Sep 28 to Oct 2

    GP1: from interview to headset

    We talked with an older adult about memory and their daily routines, prototyped a 9-screen memory activity on paper, built it in ForgeXR, and played it on the Meta Quest 3. Full write-up on the GP1 page.

    What we did

    • Interviewed an older adult in their 70s (Oct 1) about memory and daily routines
    • Ran the whole activity with paper cards before touching the authoring tool
    • Built a 9-screen ForgeXR lesson: watch and repeat a color pattern, match colored shapes to numbers, and put the steps of making tea in order
    • Recorded a narrated playthrough of all nine screens in the headset, then fixed the problems we found in a revised version

    What we learned

    • Memory trouble shows up most in everyday routines, so practice should be built around familiar tasks like making tea.
    • Our participant wished some kind of help existed, which pushed us toward a calm, supportive design with no timers or penalties.

    Next week

    • Start GP2: needfinding, problem definition, and personas
