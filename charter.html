Team Charter

Our team contract: how we meet, decide, share work, and hold each other accountable. Signed September 23, 2026.

The basics

Team name
Pelmanists
Broad problem area
Using VR and the memory game Simon to help older adults train their memory.
Team members
Caden Dirnberger, Salvador Lomeli Rodriguez, Ethan Bucy

Team procedures

Regular meetings
Tuesdays at 11:20, Ford Hall, first floor.
Communication
Google Chat, for meeting notices, announcements, updates, reminders, and problems.
Decision making
By consensus first. If we're deadlocked, by majority vote.
Meeting agendas
Whoever makes the first suggestion sets the agenda, immediately.
Record keeping
Agendas and minutes are self-reported in a shared Google Doc.

Team expectations

Work quality

Project standards
Work is complete once the rubric is satisfied.
How we get there
  • Diligent note-taking and clear workflows
  • Reviewing work before final submission

Team participation

Cooperation and equal workload
We all show up to the meeting and divide tasks evenly.
Including every voice
Everyone can submit an idea before the team moves on.
Staying on task
We follow the proposed agenda.
Leadership
Informal. Whoever speaks up first leads.

Personal accountability

Attendance, punctuality, participation
100%.
Responsibility for assignments and deadlines
Every member completes their portion of the work and can ask for help if needed.
Communication
Acknowledge Google Chat messages within 24 hours on weekdays.
Commitment to team decisions and tasks
100%.

Consequences

If someone doesn't follow procedures or meet expectations
Sent to jail.

Agreement

By signing, each of us agrees that:

  • We participated in formulating the standards, roles, and procedures in this contract.
  • We are obligated to abide by these terms and conditions.
  • If we don't abide by them, we will face the consequences stated above, which may include being removed from the team.
